Welcome to the **Couchers.org Community Builder Toolkit**! 

While the [Community Builder Guide](/documents/community-builder) outlines your overarching role and initial setup tasks, this Toolkit is your practical, day-to-day manual for hosting great events, welcoming members, sustaining values-aligned growth, and handling common community scenarios.

---

## 1. Growing & Supporting Your Community Day-to-Day

As a Community Builder, your two most important ongoing goals are **growing your community sustainably** and **supporting local members**. Here are the most effective ways to do that:

### A. Organize, Host, and Encourage Events
Events are the heartbeat of local couch surfing communities. You can organize them yourself or encourage other local members to host! Great formats include:
- **Casual Socials:** Coffee mornings, park hangouts, or drinks at a quiet cafe or pub.
- **Activity Meetups:** City walking tours, bike rides, group hikes, museum visits, or board game nights.
- **Food & Culture:** Potlucks, communal cooking, recipe exchanges, picnics, or BBQs.
- **Multi-Day Gatherings:** Regional "[Couch Crashes](https://couchers.org/crashes)", weekend campouts (*couch surfing with Mother Nature!*), or seasonal city invasions.

### B. Welcome New Members
A warm welcome turns a passive sign-up into an active community member:
- **Find New Joiners:** Check your community page or search your local area on the map to spot newer members.
- **Send a Welcome Note:** Send a friendly message welcoming them to your local community, inviting them to upcoming events or discussions, and pointing them to the [Help Center](https://help.couchers.org/) if they are new to the platform.

### C. Answer Questions & Connect Resources
- While our [Help Center](https://help.couchers.org/) covers common questions, members often prefer talking to a real person in their area.
- When answering a member's question via direct message or on the community page, include a link to the relevant Help Center article so they know where to look in the future.
- **Not sure of the answer?** Never guess on policy or technical issues! Reach out to your CBPMs at [community.building@couchers.org](mailto:community.building@couchers.org) or direct technical/account inquiries to [support@couchers.org](mailto:support@couchers.org).

### D. Keep Your Community Page Fresh
- **Community Photo:** Ensure your community banner photo represents your city/region well and does not contain promotional text or watermarks and is licensed to be used freely.
- **Short Description (50–80 words):** Appears under "General information" on the "Overview" tab ([example](https://couchers.org/community/64/madison)). To update this, email your new text to [community.building@couchers.org](mailto:community.building@couchers.org) with your community name in the subject line.
- **Long Description ("Local Info" tab):** Editable directly by you ([example](https://couchers.org/community/64/madison/info)). Keep it stocked with public transit tips from airports/stations, safety advice, emergency phone numbers (police, ambulance, fire), and local cultural norms. Ask your local community for suggestions so it becomes a collaborative resource!

### E. Uphold Community Values & Report Violations
- **Always Report, Never Confront:** If you witness or learn about any violation of our [Community Rules](/rules) or [Terms of Service](/terms), report it immediately using the **green flag icon** (available on profiles, events, discussions, comments, chats and photos) or by emailing **[support@couchers.org](mailto:support@couchers.org)**. Include the username of the person involved, full context, and what happened. To protect yourself and maintain neutrality, **do not** confront offenders directly, let the Trust & Safety team handle moderation.
- **Support Affected Members:** If a community member tells you they experienced a rule violation or safety issue, offer empathetic support and encourage them to submit a report via the green flag tool or [support@couchers.org](mailto:support@couchers.org). Even if they prefer not to report it themselves, if the incident involves a safety risk or serious violation, **you must send a report to [support@couchers.org](mailto:support@couchers.org)** so our team can protect the wider community.

### F. Share Platform Feedback & Meta Discussions
- As you use Couchers.org and talk with local members, you will gather valuable insights on bugs, feature requests, and community sentiment. Share these regularly with your CBPMs!
- When discussing platform topics within your local community, always keep member privacy and our core values in mind, never publicly discuss specific moderation incidents or individual users.

### G. Spot Future Volunteers & CBs
- **Co-Leaders:** If you meet an enthusiastic, values-aligned member in your area, encourage them to apply on our [Community Builder Application Page](/volunteering/community-builder) so you can build a team!
- **Platform Volunteers:** Couchers.org is a 501(c)(3) non-profit built entirely by volunteers. If you meet software engineers, designers, marketers, or translators at your events, encourage them to check out our [Volunteer Page](https://couchers.org/volunteer)!

## 2. Practical Tips for Organizing In-Person Events

Planning a local meetup? Use this checklist to set your event up for success:

1. **Pick an Accessible Day & Time**
   - Avoid standard weekday working hours (like Tuesday at 10:00 AM) unless your event is specifically aimed at shift workers or travelers in transit.
   - Mid-week evenings (e.g., Wednesday or Thursday) or weekend afternoons often work better than late Friday/Saturday nights, unless you give plenty of advance notice.

2. **Choose the Right Venue & Atmosphere**
   - **Space Flexibility:** Attendance at free community events naturally fluctuates. Pick a venue where it is easy to pull up extra chairs, having slightly too much space is always better than being overcrowded.
   - **Noise Level:** Choose a spot where people can comfortably converse without shouting over loud music.
   - **Inclusivity & Restrictions:** Be mindful of age limits (e.g., 21+ bars if your event welcomes all adults aged 18+), smoking areas, and pets (for attendees with allergies).
   - **Cost:** Favor free public spaces (parks, plazas) or casual venues with no mandatory minimum spend so travelers and locals on any budget feel welcome. Private homes also work great for potlucks!

3. **Have a Bad-Weather Backup Plan**
   - If your event is outdoors, always pick a nearby indoor backup spot (like a covered market or large cafe) and clearly state the backup plan in the event description.

4. **Include Clear Transit, Parking & Accessibility Info**
   - Clearly describe how to find the exact meeting spot (e.g., *"On the lawn near the north fountain"* or *"Upstairs at the long wooden tables"*).
   - List the nearest public transit stops, bike/car parking availability, and step-free/wheelchair accessibility details on the event page.

5. **Promote Your Event Effectively**
   - **Invite the Community:** Once your event is published on Couchers.org, click the **"Invite the community"** button on the event page to request an email notification to all members in your area. ([Learn how "Invite the community" works here](https://help.couchers.org/hc/couchersorg-help-center/articles/1720304409-how-does-the-invite-the-community-feature-work)).
   - **Personal Invites:** Share the event link with new members you recently welcomed or active locals.
   - **Cross-Posting:** If appropriate, share the event in local hospitality exchange or travel groups, just be respectful of each group's rules and avoid spamming unrelated pages.
   - **Major Regional/National Events:** Planning a Couch Crash or multi-day gathering? Send a request on our [Crash Calendar](https://couchers.org/crashes) and we will promote your event.

6. **Bring a Couchers.org Sign**
   - First-time attendees are often nervous about approaching the wrong table! Print out a Couchers.org table sign (or prop it up on a tablet) so newcomers can spot your group immediately.
   - **Take a look at our [Official Merch](https://shop.couchershq.org/) (Shirts, flags, and more)**. Tip: Ask for a discount code!

7. **Build a Consistent Routine**
   - Hosting a recurring event on a predictable schedule (e.g., *"First Wednesday of every month"* or *"Every second Thursday"*) builds long-term habit and momentum, even if the first few meetups are small!

## 3. Community Growth & Our Values: Quality Over Quantity

When growing your local community, remember that **we prioritize cultural health and safety over raw user numbers.**

Not everyone is a good fit for non-transactional hospitality exchange. People who intend to use the platform for dating, hookups, commercial promotion, or taking advantage of free lodging without engaging in cultural exchange do not belong on Couchers.org. Do not spend your time or energy trying to recruit or convert them.

At this stage of our growth, focus your outreach on welcoming people who genuinely share (or are eager to embrace) our core values. Building a high-trust foundation now is what allows our community to scale safely for years to come.
