Thank you for your interest in becoming a Community Builder (CB)!

Community Builders (CBs) are more than just ambassadors. The Couchers.org platform is centered around local communities, you are the stewards who help bring them to life. CBs are the volunteers responsible for growing the platform organically through local engagement: organizing or supporting events, sparking discussions, welcoming new members, and upholding our culture of trust and safety. Simply put, your mission is to **help build a thriving, values-driven local couch surfing community** on Couchers.org.

Our core growth strategy centers on expanding and empowering our global network of Community Builders. Rather than chasing raw user numbers, we prioritize sustainable, values-aligned growth. By connecting with fellow hospitality-exchange organizers, onboarding your local community, and acting as the bridge between local members and the Couchers.org volunteer team, you ensure that Couchers.org remains a platform truly built by and for the global couch surfing community.

> **Looking for practical tips?** Alongside this guide, be sure to read our **[Community Builder Toolkit](/documents/community-builder-toolkit)** for step-by-step advice on hosting great events, welcoming members, and handling day-to-day scenarios.

---

## About Being a Community Builder

**On the Couchers.org platform**, your responsibilities are to steward your local community page, keep it active through discussions and events, maintain helpful public information for locals and travelers, and help ensure that community activity aligns with our [Community Rules](/rules) and [Terms of Service](/terms). 

Because you will represent Couchers.org, your actions shape how members perceive the platform. To ensure everyone feels welcomed and safe, please remain **neutral, welcoming, and constructive**. Furthermore, you are the vital bridge between our members and the core team: gathering and forwarding community feedback to your Community Builder Program Managers (CBPMs) ensures the project stays community-driven and led.

**Outside of Couchers.org**, you play a key role in local outreach, bringing your local hospitality community onto the platform and sharing Couchers.org through word of mouth, local events, online spaces, and local media.

## Core Platform Tasks

When you first take on a community (or join an existing local CB team), start with these foundational steps:

1. **Short Description:** Check the "General information" section on your community's "Overview" tab ([example](https://couchers.org/community/64/madison)). If it is missing or needs an update, write a concise overview (**50–80 words**). **Email the text to [community.building@couchers.org](mailto:community.building@couchers.org)** with your community name in the subject line (e.g., *Description Update: Madison*) and our team will update it for you.
   ![Screenshot of the Madison community short description](/img/docs/community_builder_short_description.jpg)

2. **Long Description ("Local Info" Page):** Review and update the text and picture on your community's "Local info" page ([example](https://couchers.org/community/64/madison/info)). You can edit this directly on the page! Include practical tips for travelers and locals—such as public transit advice, neighborhood tips, regular meetup customs, and emergency numbers.
   ![Screenshot of how to edit long description](/img/docs/community_builder_long_description.jpg)

3. **Write an Introductory Post:** Start a discussion thread on your community page introducing yourself. Let members know you are their local Community Builder and a go-to person for questions, event ideas, or platform feedback. Be sure to reply when members comment to build momentum! Feel free to adapt this template:
   > *"Hey everyone! I'm [Name], and I'm excited to be a Community Builder here in [City/Region]. I’ve been couch surfing for [X] years and love connecting travelers and locals. I'm here to help grow our local community, organize events, and help you get the most out of Couchers.org. Feel free to reach out if you have any questions or ideas for local meetups! Drop a comment below to introduce yourself. What's your favorite local spot in [City], or where is the coolest place you've ever traveled?"*

4. **Host Events & Spark Discussions:** Keep your community hub alive by posting regular events or discussion prompts. Check out the [Community Builder Toolkit](/documents/community-builder-toolkit) for event ideas and planning checklists.

## Outreach Tasks

1. **Recruit Fellow Community Builders:** Keep an eye out for experienced, welcoming, and well-connected couch surfers in your city (to co-lead alongside you) or in other cities. Your personal network is the best starting point, as well as active event organizers from other hospitality exchange and travel communities. Community Builders should reflect the diversity of our global community. Actively encourage people across diverse ages, genders, backgrounds, and ethnicities to get involved.
2. **Event Outreach:** Attend local travel, language exchange, or hospitality events (such as Couchsurfing™, BeWelcome, Trustroots, Meetup, Nomadtable, or local Facebook/WhatsApp groups) and organically share what we are building at Couchers.org.
3. **Digital Outreach:** Spread the word on your social media channels and in relevant travel forums. Where possible and welcomed by group admins, encourage existing local couch surfing groups (on Facebook, WhatsApp, or Telegram) to include Couchers.org in their group title and description (like the [Chicago community](https://www.facebook.com/groups/cschicago/)).
   ![Screenshot of facebook couch surfing group in Chicago that includes Couchers.org in the title](/img/docs/community_builder_chicago_fb.jpg)
4. **Media & Creator Outreach:** Connect with local news outlets, neighborhood blogs, travel podcasters, and content creators who care about community, slow travel, or non-profit tech, and share the Couchers.org story with them.

## Reporting Users and Content

To keep both our members and you safe, **Community Builders should remain neutral and never confront rule-breakers directly.** Instead, we rely on you to be our eyes and ears on the platform and report any activity, content, or behavior that violates our [Community Rules](/rules), [Terms of Service](/terms), or the spirit of the platform.

* **How to Report:** Use the **green flag icon** next to events, discussions, comments, photos, and user profiles to submit a report directly to our Trust & Safety team.
* **Email Escalation:** If a situation cannot be captured via the flag icon or requires additional context/screenshots, please email **[support@couchers.org](mailto:support@couchers.org)** with the username(s) involved and full details.

## Future of Community Builders

In the future, Community Builders will have the opportunity to apply to become **Community Moderators**, enabling trusted local leaders to help moderate content directly within their communities. This is part of our long-term vision for a distributed moderation model, where responsibility is shared with local communities rather than strictly centralized. While becoming a moderator will always be optional for Community Builders, we look forward to collaborating with interested CBs as these moderation tools are built. Until then, please continue to report all issues via the green flag tool or [support@couchers.org](mailto:support@couchers.org).
