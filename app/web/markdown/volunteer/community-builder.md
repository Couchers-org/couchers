## Position Description

**This is a local volunteer position**

We are recruiting Community Builders (CBs) around the world to help grow, nurture, and support local Couchers.org communities through active engagement. As a Community Builder, you are more than just an ambassador, you are a local leader who connects members in your area and represents your community's voice to the broader Couchers.org team.

This is an unpaid volunteer position where you will collaborate closely with our Community Builder Program Managers (CBPMs) and fellow CBs worldwide.

### Mandatory Duties

Before applying, please thoroughly read the [Community Builder Guide](/documents/community-builder) and the [Community Builder Toolkit](/documents/community-builder-toolkit) to understand the role and day-to-day expectations.

As you represent your community and Couchers.org as a Community Builder, you are expected to do the following:

- **Represent Couchers.org:** Positively reflect our core values, non-profit mission, and guidelines across all online and in-person interactions.
- **Uphold Rules & Community Guidelines:** Follow the [Couchers.org Community Guidelines and Rules](/rules) and educate members when necessary.
- **Report Violations:** Actively and promptly report any rule violations or potential issues to the Safety & Trust Team using the in-app green flag reporting tool or by emailing [safety@couchers.org](mailto:support@couchers.org).
- **Direct & Resource Members:** Point community members toward the appropriate resources when needed (e.g., [Help Center](https://help.couchers.org/), Safety & Support Teams, Community Pages, Features, or Account Settings).
- **Collaborate with CBPMs:** Work closely with Community Builder Program Managers by sharing local updates, community challenges, and member feedback on platform features.

### Elective Duties

Because every local community has different needs, you may choose how you focus your local engagement. You are expected to regularly participate in **at least one** of the following:

- **Organize Local Events:** On a regular basis, plan and host community gatherings, meetups, or activities relevant to couch surfing and share them on Couchers.org.
- **Manage Community Pages:** Maintain your local community page by keeping local information updated, and report any spam or other questionable events or discussions on the community page.
- **Active Outreach:** Spread the word about Couchers.org locally through word of mouth, travel groups, or social media.
- **Active Hosting & Surfing:** Lead by example by maintaining high activity on the platform as a host or by meeting up with travelers who are visiting your community.

## Who Can Apply?

To ensure our Community Builders are active, trusted, and familiar with how the platform works, we split our criteria into **what is required before you apply** and **what is helpful (but optional)**.

### 1. Required Before Applying
Please make sure you have completed these steps on your Couchers.org account before submitting your application:

- **A Complete Profile:** You have a [fully filled-out profile](https://help.couchers.org/hc/couchersorg-help-center/articles/1725919197-how-do-i-create-a-great-profile), including thoughtful "About Me" and "My Home" sections, and a clear profile photo that meets our [photo guidelines](https://help.couchers.org/hc/couchersorg-help-center/articles/1777852193-test).
- **Local Page Activity:** You have already interacted on your local Community Page (by joining or creating events, participating in discussions, or both).
- **Couch Surfing Experience:** You have experience with couch surfing (hosting, surfing, or hosting/attending community meetups on Couchers.org or other couch surfing platforms) and genuinely share our community values.


### 3. Preferred (Bonus)
- **Strong Verification (SV):** Having a [Strong Verification badge](https://couchers.org/badges/strong_verification) helps build extra trust on the platform. We encourage CBs to complete SV if they can, though it isn't a strict requirement.
- **Event Organizing Experience:** Prior experience hosting local meetups, hangouts, or group activities (or a strong excitement to organize your first one!).

---

### Time & Commitment Expectations

- **Time Commitment:** 1–2 hours per week on average.
- **Duration:** 6-month minimum commitment to help build lasting momentum in your local community.
- **Teamwork:** Willingness to collaborate with fellow CBs, CBPMs, and the Safety & Trust Team.
- **Volunteer Status:** This is an unpaid volunteer position. Couchers.org is a 501(c)(3) non-profit built and run entirely by volunteers.

## How to Apply

1. Fill out the [Community Builder Application Form](https://forms.monday.com/forms/1e84199578bc028b282a88b9e325d646?r=use1).
2. Our team will review your application and get back to you as soon as possible via email.
3. Once accepted, we will get you added to your community page and onboarded.
