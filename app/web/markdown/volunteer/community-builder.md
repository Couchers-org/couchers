## Position Description

**This is a local volunteer position**

We are recruiting Community Builders (CBs) around the world to help grow, nurture, and support local Couchers.org communities through active engagement. As a Community Builder, you are more than just an ambassador, you are a local leader who connects members in your area and represents your community's voice to the broader Couchers.org team.

This is an unpaid volunteer position where you will collaborate closely with our Community Builder Program Managers (CBPMs) and fellow CBs worldwide.

### Mandatory Duties

Before applying, please thoroughly read the [Community Builder Guide](/documents/community-builder) and the [Community Builder Toolkit](/documents/community-builder-toolkit) to understand the role and day-to-day expectations.

As a representative of Couchers.org, you are expected to uphold the following baseline commitments:

- **Represent Couchers.org:** Positively reflect our core values, non-profit mission, and guidelines across all online and in-person interactions.
- **Collaborate with CBPMs:** Work closely with Community Builder Program Managers by sharing local updates, community challenges, and member feedback on platform features.
- **Uphold Community Standards:** Adhere to and promote the [Couchers.org Community Guidelines and Rules](/rules) and [Terms of Service](/terms).
- **Report Violations:** Actively and promptly report any safety issues or rule violations to the Support & Safety team using the in-app green flag reporting tool or by emailing [support@couchers.org](mailto:support@couchers.org).
- **Direct & Resource Members:** Point community members toward the appropriate resources when needed (e.g., [Help Center](https://help.couchers.org/), Support Team, Community Page, or Account Settings).

### Elective Duties

Because every local community has different needs, you may choose how you focus your local engagement. You are expected to regularly participate in **at least one** of the following:

- **Organize Local Events:** Plan and host community gatherings, meetups, or activities on a regular basis.
- **Manage Community Pages:** Maintain your local community hub by keeping local information updated, sparking discussions, and welcoming new members.
- **Active Outreach:** Spread the word about Couchers.org locally through word of mouth, travel groups, or local media.
- **Active Hosting & Surfing:** Lead by example by maintaining high activity on the platform as a host or traveler.

## Who Can Apply?

To ensure our Community Builders are active, trusted, and familiar with how the platform works, we split our criteria into **what is required before you apply** and **what is helpful (but optional)**.

### 1. Required Before Applying
Please make sure you have completed these steps on your Couchers.org account before submitting your application:

- **A Complete Profile:** You have a [fully filled-out profile](https://help.couchers.org/hc/couchersorg-help-center/articles/1725919197-how-do-i-create-a-great-profile), including thoughtful "About Me" and "My Home" sections, and a clear profile photo that meets our [photo guidelines](https://help.couchers.org/hc/couchersorg-help-center/articles/1777852193-test).
- **Local Page Activity:** You have already interacted on your local Community Page (by posting or replying in discussions, joining or creating events, or both).
- **Hospitality Exchange Experience:** You have experience with non-transactional hospitality exchange (hosting, surfing, or hosting/attending community meetups on Couchers.org or other hospitality platforms) and genuinely share our community values.

### 2. What We Look For in a Candidate
- **Community Mindset:** Enthusiasm for bringing people together—whether as a local guide, host, discussion starter, or community advocate.
- **Collaborative & Welcoming Spirit:** Empathy, patience, and constructive communication, especially when helping members navigate an evolving, volunteer-built platform.
- **Proactive Ownership & Initiative:** A self-starter attitude with the drive to spot needs, propose ideas, and take action without waiting for step-by-step instructions.
- **Independent Problem-Solving:** Ability to navigate ambiguity, prioritize tasks autonomously, and follow through on commitments in a decentralized, remote environment.

### 3. Preferred (Bonus)
- **Strong Verification (SV):** Having a [Strong Verification badge](https://couchers.org/badges/strong_verification) helps build extra trust on the platform. We encourage CBs to complete SV if they can, though we gladly make exceptions if you don't hold a compatible biometric passport.
- **Event Organizing Experience:** Prior experience hosting local meetups, hangouts, or group activities (or a strong excitement to organize your first one!).

---

### Time & Commitment Expectations

- **Time Commitment:** 2–5 hours per week on average.
- **Duration:** 6-month minimum commitment to help build lasting momentum in your local community.
- **Teamwork:** Willingness to stay in touch with fellow CBs, CBPMs, and the Trust & Safety team.
- **Volunteer Status:** This is an unpaid role. Couchers.org is a 501(c)(3) non-profit built and run entirely by volunteers.

## How to Apply

1. Fill out the [Community Builder Application Form](https://forms.monday.com/forms/1e84199578bc028b282a88b9e325d646?r=use1).
2. Our Community Builder Program Managers (CBPMs) will review your application and get back to you as soon as possible.
3. Once accepted, we will get you onboarded and connected with the team and fellow CBs in your region!
