We are making two big changes to hosting preferences.

## Why we're making changes to this

In the past we used to have three options: "can host", "may host", and "can't host". Although the first and last options were pretty clear, the middle option, "may host", was very confusing.

Some people used it to indicate that they are open to hosting but are picky, others used it to indicate that they are open to hosting but were hoping to show up lower in search results (such as to give fresh members a chance to host), and yet there was another group that felt they needed to indicate willingness to host to increase their chances to be hosted, and so chose "may host" even when they had no intention to host anyone.

We spent some time digging into the data and trying to understand the usage patterns of the community, and we came up with a new system where we now separate your openness to hosting from your keenness to host.

## Openness to hosting: removing "may host" and clarifying hosting statuses

![Screenshot of the hosting statuses]()

We are clarifying the meaning of hosting statuses, and removing the "may host" label entirely. The new statuses are:

> **Open to requests**: [I'm/this user is] open to receiving hosting requests for consideration from surfers

> **Not open to requests**: [I'm/this user is] not currently open to receiving hosting requests

Additionally there will be a third option that cannot be selected by a host, but if they choose "Open to requests" and are not responsive, we will set their status to this:

> **Unresponsive**: this user is open to requests but has not been responsive or active lately
