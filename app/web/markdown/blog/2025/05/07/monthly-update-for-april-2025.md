Buckle up for our monthly development update! Read about the new features and changes in April 2025.

**The following are new additions:**

* The **search results list view** on the map page lets you see many search results at once (by [Nicole](https://couchers.org/user/unsettleddown))
* **New search filters** have been added to allow you to easily filter inactive and empty profiles, or search for people who are keen to meet up (by [Nicole](https://couchers.org/user/unsettleddown))
* A **Notifications Feed** so you don’t miss any important messages (by [Nicole](https://couchers.org/user/unsettleddown))
* A **Donations Portal** to easily manage and review your donations (by [Aapeli](https://couchers.org/user/aapeli))
* **Strong Verification** has been released into account settings after testing in feature preview (by [Aapeli](https://couchers.org/user/aapeli))
* **Username & profile link** on profile pages to easily share profiles (by [Pablo](https://couchers.org/user/pcolt86))

**These things have changed:**

* Complete overhaul of the **Map Search** which now shows the total number of results found (and how many are currently displayed). You can also expand the search results area & minimize the map so you can see more results at once. There are also improved filter options to find who you are looking for in a snap (by [Nicole](https://couchers.org/user/unsettleddown))
* New **Roadmap design** to easily see what we've been working on and what's coming next (by [Chris](https://couchers.org/user/chrisk))
* Improved **Event Time Picker**, it doesn’t just look better, it’s also easier to use (by [Colleen](https://couchers.org/user/colleen))
* Started the **Blog Revamp** to keep you better informed (by [Aapeli](https://couchers.org/user/aapeli), [Chris](https://couchers.org/user/chrisk), [Colleen](https://couchers.org/user/colleen))
* Updated and added more **[Help Center](https://help.couchers.org) pages** to provide clarity and answer more frequently asked questions (by [Jesse](https://couchers.org/user/jesse))

## Development stats

**In April 2025 Couchers.org had:**

* **15 Developers** contribute in
* **209 Changes** that changed
* **283 Files** with **6068 Additions** and **3670 Deletions**.

None of this would be possible without our volunteers working in all areas of the project. The development team would like to thank the rest of the contributors for making our work possible.
