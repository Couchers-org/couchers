We’re excited to share a milestone the community has been waiting for:

The official [Couchers Merchandise Shop](https://couchers.org/shop) is now live!

Couchers.org has grown through the passion and hard work of volunteers across the world: developers, designers, translators, community builders, hosts, and travelers. Now, for the first time, you can represent that spirit in your everyday life with high-quality merch created specifically for the community.

Whether you’re attending a meetup, hosting travelers, or exploring new places, Couchers.org merch (merchandise) is a fun way to show your support while helping grow our community and platform.

## Why a Merchandise Shop?

When you purchase merch from the shop, the Couchers.org non-profit makes a bit of money and you bring visibility to our community. The merch shop therefore helps support:

* Long-term sustainability of the platform
* Server hosting and development costs
* Community initiatives, outreach, and events
* Continued growth of our global community

Every purchase directly contributes to keeping Couchers alive, improving it continuously, and making it accessible to all.

## What You’ll Find in the Shop

The Couchers Merch Shop launches with a curated collection designed for travelers, volunteers, and anyone who loves being part of this community.

**Clothing**
* **Unisex Hoodie** - cozy, classic, embroidered with the Couchers logo
* **Unisex Premium Oversized Hoodie** - an extra-comfy, high-quality option for colder days

**Accessories**
* **Cap (Embroidered)** - versatile, stylish, and ideal for travel
* **Pin Button Set 1** - cute collectible pins featuring Couchers graphics

**Home & Living**
* **Sticker Sheet 1** - perfect for laptops, water bottles, journals, and travel gear
* **Large Couchers Flag (154.94 × 96.52 cm)** - great for drawing attention at meetups, events, or decorating your space

Everything is made on demand, ensuring sustainable production with no unnecessary waste.

## Made for the Community, by the Community

Every product reflects the creativity and dedication of Couchers volunteers who contributed their design skills, ideas, and time to make this shop possible.

Wearing Couchers merch isn’t just about showing off a logo.

**It’s about celebrating meaningful travel, connection, and global hospitality.**

You’re helping build momentum for a platform that remains free, open, and community-first.
