You've got a couch. We've still got a server bill. So here it is, our one and only donation ask of the year. We promise not to make it weird.

**TL;DR**: the servers are hungry. You can feed them [here](https://couchers.org/donate) 🍪

Couchers.org is a registered 501(c)(3) non-profit, built by volunteers and funded by the people who use it. No investors, no paywalls, no ads. That's how we want to keep it, and it only works if the community helps cover the costs.

## Last year, you showed up

Our first ever donation drive raised about **$2,000** in just a few weeks, with more donations from more people than we'd ever had before. Thanks to you, we closed 2025 with donations covering *all* of our running costs for the first time. Thank you! ❤️

## This year, Couchers took off

* 🎉 **Over 37,000 new members** since this time last year, growing our community by more than half. We passed [100,000 Couchers](https://couchers.org/blog/2026/11/12/100-000-couchers-milestone-reached) in November, with more than 25,000 joining in the last six months alone
* 📱 **Couchers is in your pocket:** our [iOS and Android apps](https://couchers.org/blog/2026/05/15/mobile-app-release) launched in May, our most requested feature ever
* 🏠 When Couchsurfing.com™ [pushed an update that sent thousands looking elsewhere](https://couchers.org/blog/2026/05/09/couch-surfing-community-looking-at-us), you made them feel at home
* 💬 **One inbox** for all your chats and host requests, and a [smarter dashboard](https://couchers.org/blog/2026/10/15/version-1.4.0-release) with your upcoming stays and local events up front
* 🏕️ The [Couch Crash Calendar](https://couchers.org/crashes), so you can plan your route around gatherings, invasions and camps
* 🌍 **Couchers speaks your language:** this time last year, 3 languages were more than 80% translated. Now it's 9, with Italian, Ukrainian, Dutch, Brazilian Portuguese, and Simplified and Traditional Chinese joining German, Spanish and Russian, all thanks to our volunteer translators
* 🌙 Dark mode and profile photo galleries
* 🏙️ Lots more city and country communities, so you can find your people wherever you are
* 🛍️ The [merch shop](https://shop.couchershq.org/) opened, so you can finally wear the couch

All in all, we checked off **30 items** on our [2026 roadmap](https://couchers.org/roadmap), with 5 more well underway.

## Coming up in 2027

* 🧳 **Public Trips**, one of our most requested features: post where you're going and when, and let local hosts offer to host you. We're wrapping up the final tasks and testing now, and plan to release it very early in 2027
* 📬 **Postal address verification:** we mail you a postcard with a code, you get a Verified Address badge
* 🔁 **Recurring events**, so your weekly meetup only needs setting up once
* 🌐 **Public events** that you can share with friends who aren't on Couchers yet
* 🗳️ **Have your say:** a feature request board, so you can tell us what to build next
* 🗺️ **Better location and map search**, so it's easier to find hosts and locals exactly where you're headed
* 👤 **More intuitive profiles** that are easier to fill in and give a better sense of who you'd be staying with
* 🛋️ **"My Home" photos**, so hosts can show off the couch (or spare room) you'll actually be sleeping on
* 🌱 **Authentic, holistic growth:** we don't just want *more* members, we want the *right* ones. People who care about genuine, non-transactional cultural exchange, and who'll host, meet up and look out for each other the way this community always has

Follow along on our [roadmap](https://couchers.org/roadmap).

None of this happens without **volunteers and supporters like you**.

## We're aiming for $15,000 by January 2nd

Your couch is free. Our servers aren't. This year we're aiming for **$15,000** in donations.

Thanks to everyone who's already given this year, we're well on our way: as of December 1st, we've raised roughly **$10,000**, so we're aiming for roughly **$5,000 more** by January 2nd. You can [watch the thermometer fill up here](https://couchers.org/donate).

## What we need it for

A bigger community costs more to run. Your donation pays for:

* 🖥️ **Servers and hosting**, our biggest cost, which grows with every new member
* 📱 **The mobile apps**, which cost us each time we build and ship a new release
* 🛠️ **Tools for our volunteer developers**, so they can keep building instead of fighting their tools
* 🌱 **Careful growth**, reaching more travelers and hosts who share our values

We keep costs low by relying on volunteers and open-source software, so every dollar goes a long way. And we show our work: [here's exactly where every dollar went in 2025](https://couchers.org/blog/2026/02/16/couchers-inc-financials-2025).

## Can you chip in?

Even a few bucks helps: last year, most of our donors gave $10 or less. A small **monthly donation** helps even more, so we can plan ahead.

Giving more than $1,000? Email us at [benefactor@couchers.org](mailto:benefactor@couchers.org) and we'll set up a bank transfer with lower fees, so more of your gift reaches us.

Thanks for making 2026 the year Couchers really took off. Here's to keeping it cozy, open and ad-free.
