This is a very short summary of the 2025 financials of Couchers, Inc.: the 501(c)3 non-profit that supports Couchers.org. Check out also last year's post on [2024 financials](/blog/2025/04/20/couchers-inc-financials-2024).

**Couchers is aiming to raise $5000 in 2026** to pay for hosting the platform, and supporting the development and growth of the community. This is **less than 8 cents per user**. We'll reach this goal if just **one in a thousand users chip in with $10!**

You can donate to support the project by logging in and going to the [Donate](/donate) page!

## What we spent money on

Couchers spent **$2988.34** in 2025, equivalent to **4.5 cents per user**, the breakdown of costs is as follows:

* Amazon Web Services (AWS) &mdash; **$2024.92** (67.7%): hosting of the Couchers.org platform and development/support/moderation tools
* Printful &mdash; **$379.73** (12.7%): producing Couchers.org [branded merch](https://couchers.org/shop) (these are largely offset by donations in excess of merch fees)
* Vercel &mdash; **$201.07** (6.7%): hosting developer previews of the platform
* TechSoup &mdash; **$95.00** (3.2%): nonprofit technology services and software licenses
* Zoom &mdash; **$75.78** (2.5%): videoconferencing platform for volunteer meetings
* Expo.dev &mdash; **$75.00** (2.5%): a development tool to automatically build and deploy preview versions of the native app
* Banking Fees &mdash; **$72.00** (2.4%): bank account maintenance fees
* Calendly &mdash; **$40.50** (1.4%): meeting scheduling platform we use to organize volunteer onboarding calls
* Twilio &mdash; **$20.00** (0.7%): SMS services for phone verification
* Porkbun &mdash; **$4.07** (0.1%): internet domain registrar
* Google Cloud &mdash; **$0.27** (<0.1%): cloud services

## Where we received money from

Couchers received **$6292.37** via donations from the community in 2025 after Stripe fees (our payment processor). We processed **$6510.86** of donations and paid fees of **$218.49** in 2025 (note that there is a slight discrepancy between Stripe and our bank account due to timing). That is, we paid 3.4% in payment processing fees. Larger donations have a lower per-transaction fee (if you would like to make a large donation, we can issue a one-off invoice for a bank transfer that has practically no fee).

There were 183 unique donors. 114 donors donated $10 or less, 54 donors donated more than $10 but $50 or less, and 15 donors donated more than $50. The six donors who are on the board donated 53% of the total. Aapeli and Itsi donated $1000 each at the beginning of the year as we were running out of cash, luckily our donation drive towards the end of the year brought in more (in our [v1.2 release post](/blog/2026/02/05/couchers-v1.2-release) we mentioned we brought in roughly $2k less than the numbers in this post due to removing these two large donations from the totals there). We hope to diversify and bring in a larger share of our revenue from donors outside the board.

You can donate to Couchers via the [donation page](/donate) when logged in.

## Account balance

We started the year with **$574.67** in the bank account and ended it with **$4810.04**, with donations covering over 200% of our operating expenses.

## Outlook for 2026

We are very excited about the significant increase of our balance sheet from the end of last year, moving us into a more stable financial position.
