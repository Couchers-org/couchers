# **100,000 Couchers: How We Got Here and What Is Coming Next**

**TL;DR** Couchers.org just passed 100,000 members! It took us a year and a half to double our community from 50,000 to 100,000, with 25,000 of you joining in the last six months alone. Here is a look back at the major milestones that got us here, the community-requested features we have built along the way, and a preview of public trips.

### I**t is official: there are now over 100,000 people on Couchers.org.**

Numbers on their own are just vanity metrics unless they translate into real connections, shared meals, and actual couches to sleep on. What makes this milestone matter is that people are not just creating accounts. They are hosting, showing travelers around their hometowns, organizing local events, and turning this platform into the active, non-profit hospitality community we always knew it could be.

Here is a look at what our volunteers and community members have achieved together over the last 18 months, and where we are heading next.

### **From Beta to Native Apps: Our Biggest Milestones**

In July 2025, we officially stepped out of beta and launched [Couchers v1.0](https://couchers.org/blog/2025/07/01/releasing-couchers-v1). That release marked the point where the core couch surfing experience was solid, polished, and ready for everyone. We also shifted to a roughly quarterly release schedule (v1.1 through our recent v1.4 release), allowing our volunteer team to ship improvements at a much steadier pace.

Then came May 2026, when we reached our most requested milestone ever: releasing the [official Couchers.org mobile apps for iOS and Android](https://couchers.org/blog/2026/05/15/mobile-app-release). Having native apps in the app stores completely changed how people use the platform on the road and played a huge part in welcoming the 25,000 new members who joined over the past half year.

Around the same time, we opened the official [Couchers merchandise shop](https://shop.couchershq.org/) and saw incredible support during our community donation drives, proving that a free, open source, community-run, 501(c)(3) non-profit platform can [sustain itself without paywalls](https://couchers.org/blog/2026/02/16/couchers-inc-financials-2025) or venture capital.

### **Building What You Asked For**

Because Couchers is built by people who actually use it, our v1.x releases have focused heavily on the everyday details that make hosting and surfing smoother. Over the last year and a half, we have rolled out:

* A unified inbox and messaging overhaul: We combined chats and host requests into a single, cleaner inbox with unread filters, archiving, and the ability to message someone directly from their profile.
* A smarter dashboard: Your home screen now highlights your upcoming stays and guests right at the top, splits events into ones you are attending versus local ones in your area, and surfaces recent discussions from your most local communities.
* Better events and community navigation: We added timezone support for events, cleaner attendee lists, easy link sharing, and searchable breadcrumbs on community pages so you can jump straight to your city or region. We also added the ability to edit and delete your own discussion posts and replies.
* Dark mode and translations: We introduced dark mode for late-night trip planning and pushed translations past 80% in several major languages, with volunteer translators adding more every month.

One of our favorite additions during this stretch came directly from community feedback: the [Couch Crash Calendar](https://couchers.org/crashes). Members kept telling us they wanted a central place to discover multi-day couch surfing gatherings, invasions, and camps happening around the world without having to dig through dozens of separate city pages. We listened and built a dedicated Couch Crash Calendar where organizers can submit their multi-day events and travelers can plan their routes around upcoming crashes. Seeing events pop up from Philadelphia to Poland on that calendar has been a great reminder of how much happens offline when you give local organizers the right tools.

### **What Is Next: Public Trips Are Around the Corner**

With the mobile apps out in the world and our core messaging, dashboard, and event tools in a great spot, we are working on one of the largest missing pieces of the puzzle: Public Trips.

Soon, you will be able to post your upcoming travel plans publicly so hosts and local members in your destination can see when you are visiting and send you an invitation to stay or meet up. Whether you are planning a weekend city break or a multi-month backpacking route, public trips will take a lot of the friction out of finding hosts and connecting with locals before you even arrive. Development is well underway in our GitHub repository, and we cannot wait to get it into your hands.

### **Thank You for Being Here**

Every line of code, translated string, moderated report, and local meetup on Couchers is the work of volunteers and community members who care about keeping hospitality exchange open and genuine.

Whether you signed up back in 2020 or you are one of the 25,000 people who joined in the last six months, thank you for being part of this community. If you want to help us reach the next milestone, complete your profile, host a traveler, organize an event in your city, or consider [volunteering](https://couchers.org/volunteer) or [donating](https://couchers.org/donate) to help keep our servers running.

See you on a couch or at a Couch Crash soon!
